Grind City Coffee Xpo Throwdown

Published on Feb 1 2026

In 2024, I whipped up a quick web app to support a local coffee competition in Memphis. Users could scan a QR code on their phone and vote in each head-to-head showdown in the single elimination bracket competition. Results were displayed on a screen in real time as voting happened on a TV in the front. It was lots of fun and I'm looking forward to doing it again this year! Check out the Grind City Coffee Xpo. I love this thing. My buddy Daniel started it a few years ago with an idea and a passion. Now there are people from all over to come to this event. It's fantastic. I highly recommend everyone in Western Tennessee to check it out on March 14, 2026.
